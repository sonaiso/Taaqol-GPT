> Snapshot date: 2026-07-15.

## 1) Governing integration law

```text
Every linguistic slot reveals a bounded rational-readiness capability.
No slot may transition to a higher slot without licensed transition evidence.
```

Licensed transition criterion:

```text
SlotLicensedTransition(X -> Y)
iff
X has closed its MRK
and Y demand is declared
and trace is replayable
and residuals are visible
and rank policy allows the transition
and no forbidden shortcut is crossed.
```

This proposal preserves the repository constitutional baseline:
no hidden residuals, no ungated promotion, no direct jump from evidence/tooling to certainty/truth.
