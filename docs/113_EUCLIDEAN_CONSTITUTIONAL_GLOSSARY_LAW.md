## §0 Glossary Function

The Euclidean glossary is not a collection of terminological definitions.
It is a foundational constitutional structure that enforces:

\[
\boxed{
Definition
+
Distinction
+
LicensedDependency
}
\]

Each term must make explicit:

1. what it is,
2. what it is not,
3. what its possibility depends on,
4. what it opens after itself,
5. and what it is forbidden to open.

Canonical shape:

\[
\boxed{
Term(X)=
\langle
Genus,
Differentia,
Domain,
Boundary,
Prerequisites,
Authority,
ForbiddenEquivalences
\rangle
}
\]

## Block I — Reality, Trace, Trigger

### 1) Reality

Reality is what exists or occurs independently from the system's mere representation,
and is a possible locus for effect, correspondence, and testing.

\[
Reality\neq Representation
\]

\[
Reality\neq Claim
\]

\[
Reality\neq ModelState
\]

Constitutional roles:

\[
Reality_{Open}
\]

and

\[
Reality_{Close}.
\]

\[
\boxed{
Reality_{Open}\neq Reality_{Close}
}
\]

(functionally, even when both refer to one external object).

### 2) Trace

Trace is a preserved carrier witnessing that a prior event left a revisitable
relevant difference, without being the event itself or its interpretation.

\[
\boxed{
Trace(x)\neq x
}
\]

And:

\[
Trace\nRightarrow Meaning.
\]

Trace is a tracking precondition, not truth by itself.

### 3) Trigger

Trigger is an event/trace that moves prior capacity from latency into
activation of distinction/questioning.

\[
PriorCapacity+Trigger\to Activation.
\]

\[
Trigger\nRightarrow Answer.
\]

### 4) Prior Classified Knowledge

Structured prior knowledge is a pre-classified structure that makes distinction,
questioning, and operation possible, without granting truth to outputs.

\[
PK_{\mathrm{org}}
\Rightarrow PossibilityOfOperation
\]

\[
\boxed{
PK_{\mathrm{org}}\nRightarrow Truth.
}
\]

## Block II — Domain, Unit, Boundary, Identity

### 5) Domain

Domain is the bounded space where criteria of being, distinction, equality,
operation, and licensed relation are fixed.

\[
\boxed{
Unit(x)\Rightarrow Domain(x).
}
\]

### 6) Unit

Unit is what is distinctly established inside a domain by a boundary enabling
identity-bearing and operation-entry.

\[
\boxed{
Unit=
ExistenceInDomain+
Boundary+
Distinction.
}
\]

### 7) Boundary

Boundary determines what belongs to unit identity, what is outside, and what
crossing is licensed.

\[
Boundary=
SelfBoundary+
OperationalBoundary+
TransitionBoundary.
\]

### 8) Identity

Identity is persistence of a particular unit under declared-invariant
preservation across licensed changes, with absence of qadih difference.

\[
\boxed{
Identity=
PreservedInvariant+
LicensedChange-
QadihDifference.
}
\]

\[
Identity\neq NumericalEquality
\]

\[
Identity\neq NoChange.
\]

### 9) Invariant

Invariant is a property/relation whose preservation is required by the
transition contract for identity continuity licensing.

### 10) Qadih Difference

Qadih difference is a difference striking a necessary condition for identity,
relation, or analogy, thus blocking branch-to-origin attachment in the
claimed aspect.

\[
QadihDifference\Rightarrow BlockedIdentityOrBridge.
\]

## Block III — Possibility, Capability, Opening, Closure

### 11) Possibility

Possibility is non-impossibility of a state in a domain after its conditions
are satisfied and no blocker exists.

\[
\boxed{
Possible(x)\nRightarrow Actual(x).
}
\]

### 12) Capability

Capability is a bounded structural possibility of what a unit can accept/do
under declared conditions.

\[
Capability\neq ActualOperation.
\]

\[
Capability\neq Meaning.
\]

\[
Capability\neq RoleActualization.
\]

### 13) Opening

Opening is disclosure of a licensed possibility-position (or set) not yet
saturated.

\[
Opening\neq Closure.
\]

\[
Opening(s)\subseteq LicensedDomain(s).
\]

### 14) Saturation

Saturation is filling a required slot with a value of the licensed type.

\[
Filled\neq Saturated
\]

unless type-valid and constraint-valid.

### 15) Closure

Closure is fulfillment of current-boundary conditions with identity
preservation, trace preservation, residual visibility, and no layer-authority
exceedance.

\[
\boxed{
Closure\neq Truth.
}
\]

### 16) Minimum Complete Limit (MCE)

Minimum complete limit is the least structure sufficient to prove layer-closure
readiness for subsequent contract entry without jumps.

\[
\boxed{
MCE_i=
InternalClosure_i+
BackwardProof_i+
ForwardReadiness_i+
TriangleCoherence_i.
}
\]

## Block IV — Operation, Relation, Contract, Gate, Bridge

### 17) Operation

Operation is a transformation defined by type, input, output, invariants,
and licensed change.

\[
\omega:X\to Y
\]

Not licensed merely because executable.

### 18) Relation

Relation is an independent typed bond among parties; it does not arise from
mere existence of parties.

\[
\boxed{
Exist(A)\land Exist(B)
\nRightarrow
R(A,B).
}
\]

### 19) Relation Affordance

Relation affordance is readiness to enter a relation type, not relation
actualization.

\[
RelationAffordance\neq RelationActual.
\]

### 20) Contract

Contract is prior specification of what must hold for an operation/relation
to produce a declared-type output.

\[
Contract=
Inputs+
RelationType+
Conditions+
Preventers+
Invariants+
EvidenceRequirement+
OutputBoundary.
\]

### 21) Evidence

Evidence is trace/inferential structure supporting a specific claim within a
specific domain/scope.

\[
\boxed{
Evidence(E\mid Claim,Domain,Scope).
}
\]

\[
EvidenceAbsolute\text{ does not exist.}
\]

### 22) Gate

Gate is a decision process licensing/refusing a specific transition after
contract/evidence/rank/residual inspection.

\[
Gate\neq Bridge.
\]

### 23) Bridge

Bridge is an independent contract justifying type/domain/scope/identity/
authority changes where direct build is unlicensed.

\[
\boxed{
\Delta ConstitutionalType\neq\varnothing
\Rightarrow BridgeRequired.
}
\]

### 24) Transition

Transition is realization of a licensed operation moving a carrier from one
constitutional state to another without authority jump.

\[
\boxed{
T=
\langle
Input,Contract,Operation,Evidence,Gate,Output
\rangle.
}
\]

## Block V — Dal, Madlul, Dalalah

### 25) Dal (Signifier)

Dal is a licensed form-carrier that can enter wad/dalalah relations after
geometry exhaustion; lexical designation is not in itself.

\[
Dal\neq Meaning.
\]

### 26) Dal Geometry

Dal geometry is the total of units, slots, boundaries, ordering,
transformations, connections, affordances, and blockers of the signifier.

### 27) Closed Word Form

A dal whose required form-geometry has been exhausted with preserved units,
boundaries, transitions, and residuals.

\[
ClosedWordForm\neq LexicalMeaning.
\]

### 28) Formal Verbal Madlul

The effect of dal-closure on delimiting capability-space before lexical
assignment.

\[
FormalVerbalMadlul\neq LexicalMadlul.
\]

### 29) Madlul Possibility Space

\[
\mathcal P_M(D)
\]

is the set of madlul candidates not blocked by dal geometry.

\[
M\in\mathcal P_M(D)
\nRightarrow
D\ denotes\ M.
\]

### 30) Madlul

Madlul is a concept-unit with defined identity/boundary/type/domain, eligible
as an independent dalalah-relation party.

### 31) Wad'

Wad' is a relation establishing licensed linguistic pairing between dal and
madlul under group/term/time scope.

\[
Wad'\neq FormalCompatibility.
\]

### 32) Dalalah

Dalalah is a licensed proven relation between complete dal and independent
madlul under contract and evidence.

\[
\boxed{
Dalalah=
DalCapability+
MadlulIdentity+
SemanticContract+
Evidence.
}
\]

### 33) Mutabaqah

Dalalah of dal on the whole madlul fixed by specific wad contract.

\[
SemanticMutabaqah\neq RealityCorrespondence.
\]

### 34) Tadammun

Licensed dalalah on a fixed part of the mutabaqah whole.

\[
Tadammun
\Rightarrow
Mutabaqah+
PartOf+
IndependentEvidence.
\]

### 35) Iltizam

Licensed dalalah on a madlul outside mutabaqah boundary, with proven
independent suitable luzum relation.

\[
Iltizam
\Rightarrow
Mutabaqah+
LicensedLuzum+
Evidence.
\]

## Block VI — Nisbah, Composition, Ifadah

### 36) Nisbah

Nisbah is a directed compositional relation between complete units with
compatible affordances before ifadah.

### 37) 'Amil

'Amil is a licensed relation-affordance carrier opening a specific direction
of potential nisbah.

### 38) Ma'mul

Ma'mul is a counter-affordance carrier licensed to accept the direction opened
by the 'amil.

### 39) Composition

Composition is closure of a network of licensed relations among complete units.

\[
\boxed{
Composition=Closure(RelationNetwork).
}
\]

\[
Composition=Concatenation
\]

is forbidden equivalence.

### 40) Pre-Ifadah Formal Closure (PFC)

State where required formal relations and slots are closed with formal traces,
without claiming conceptual unified intelligibility.

\[
PFC\neq Ifadah.
\]

### 41) Conceptual Bearer

A unit that can host conceptual carrying/predication in a closed network.

### 42) Predicable

Conceptual content licit to predicate of a given bearer by a licensed relation
kind.

### 43) Conceptual Predication

Licensed relation establishing predicable-on-bearer direction, without proving
external truth of that predication.

### 44) Maqam

Maqam is the set of discourse constraints selecting/restricting/directing
interpretation within a language-opened space.

\[
\boxed{
Maqam\ restricts;\quad Maqam\ does\ not\ create\ ex\ nihilo.
}
\]

### 45) Pre-Ifadah Conceptual Closure (PCC)

\[
PCC=PreIfadahConceptualClosure
\]

Closure of formal network after conceptual remapping with adequate reference,
maqam, force, and scope.

### 46) Ifadah

Ifadah is closure of maqam-bound conceptual network into one intelligible unit
with governing relation, force, and known scope.

\[
\boxed{
Ifadah\neq Truth.
}
\]

### 47) Speech Force

Speech force is the act type performed by the ifadah unit as speech.

\[
\boxed{
IfadahIdentity=
ContentIdentity+
ForceIdentity.
}
\]

## Block VIII — Zero, One, Residual, Rank

### 56) Absolute Zero

Limit concept: absence of all domain-relevant distinguishing information,
constraints, and capability.

\[
\boxed{
AbsoluteZero
}
\]

is not a valid practical start for licensed cognition.

\[
AbsoluteZero\nRightarrow LicensedDistinction.
\]

### 57) Non-Absolute Zero

State where current determination has not started, yet it is not epistemic
nothingness; it carries prior information, possibility-rules, and constraints.

\[
\boxed{0^{+}}
\]

or

\[
Z_{prior}.
\]

\[
\boxed{
Z_{prior}
=
PriorInformation
+
DomainConstraints
+
CapabilitySpace
}
\]

### 58) Incomplete Zero

State where construction has started but MCE is not achieved; blocking
residuals or required-unsaturated slots remain.

\[
\boxed{0^{\ast}}
\]

\[
0^{\ast}
=
PartialStructure+
Residuals+
OpenRequirements.
\]

No promotion to one is licensed.

### 59) Minimal Complete One

Smallest unit whose build reached sufficient boundary proving identity,
boundary, required relations, and readiness for later contract.

\[
\boxed{1_{\min}}
\]

\[
\boxed{
1_{\min}(X)
\iff
MCE(X)=PASS.
}
\]

\[
1_{\min}^{(i)}
\nRightarrow
1_{\min}^{(i+1)}.
\]

### 60) Residual

Residual is unresolved difference/question/lack not settled by current contract
and not licit to erase.

\[
Residual\neq Error
\]

It may be:

\[
Blocking,\ Deferred,\ NonBlocking,\ Explanatory.
\]

\[
\boxed{
Residual_{out}
\supseteq
Residual_{in}
-
Resolved
+
NewResidual.
}
\]

### 61) Residual Vector

\[
\boxed{
\vec R=
(r_1,r_2,\ldots,r_n).
}
\]

Type must be preserved, not mere count.

### 62) Rank

Rank is the ceiling of what has been proven for claim/carrier in context
of a specific contract.

\[
\boxed{
Rank(Output)
\le
\bigwedge Rank(RequiredInputs).
}
\]

### 63) Promotion

Promotion is transition from rank to higher rank through independent contract,
evidence, and gate.

\[
Computation\nRightarrow Promotion.
\]

### Zero-One relation law

Not:

\[
0\to1.
\]

But:

\[
\boxed{
0^{+}
\to
0^{\ast}
\to
1_{\min}.
}
\]

No one from absolute zero.
No one from incomplete zero.
Minimal-complete one arises from constrained prior possibility after closure
conditions are fulfilled.

## Foundational-Term Definition Law

A foundational term is any term entering definitions/laws/contracts of other
terms, such that ambiguity in it propagates ambiguity.

\[
A\in Definition(B)
\]

\[
Undefined(A)\Rightarrow UnclosedDefinition(B).
\]

Definition graph:

\[
\boxed{
G_{\mathrm{Glossary}}
=
(T,E_D)
}
\]

Where:

\[
A\to B
\]

means definition of \(B\) depends on \(A\).

\[
\boxed{
Acyclic(G_{\mathrm{Glossary}})=TRUE.
}

except explicit simultaneous primitive definition.

## No Hidden Circularity Law

\[
\boxed{
\textbf{No term may be an indirect possibility-condition for defining itself.}
}
\]

Cycle:

\[
A\to B\to C\to A
\]

is forbidden unless group-primitive declaration is explicit.

\[
\boxed{

\Rightarrow
A=B
\;\lor\;

}
\]

So hidden collapses such as
`Closure/Completion`, `Meaning/Madlul`, `Relation/Binding`,
