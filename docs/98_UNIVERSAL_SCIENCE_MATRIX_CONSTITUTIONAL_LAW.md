# 98 — Universal Science Matrix (USM) Constitutional Law

> **Status:** Constitutional law document.
> **Type:** Law-only boundary (`USM-L0`).
> **Scope:** establish the bounded constitutional admission of Universal Science
> Matrix as a candidate framework; no runtime, no carriers, no validators,
> no transition execution, no science-output engine.

## §1 Purpose

`USM` is a candidate framework for methodological reasonableness universality,
not a final proof of universal scientific completeness.

It is admitted as a bounded constitutional branch surface only.

## §2 Eight-slot constitutional form

The USM constitutional tuple is fixed as:

```text
USM = <E, C, R, T, V, J, K, A>
```

Where:

- `E` = typed entities with declared identity and domain.
- `C` = typed capabilities over declared bearer types.
- `R` = typed relations over declared operands and roles.
- `T` = typed transformations under capability/invariant/condition/gate discipline.
- `V` = domain evidence surfaces with claim-type/scope mapping.
- `J` = rank-bounded judgment surfaces over evidence contracts.
- `K` = auditable knowledge objects with provenance/scope/revision posture.
- `A` = bounded applications to new cases with blockers and feedback.

## §3 Slot separation covenant

The following separations are mandatory and non-collapsible:

- `Entity != Capability`
- `Capability != Relation`
- `Relation != Transformation`
- `Transformation != Evidence`
- `Evidence != Judgment`
- `Judgment != KnowledgeObject`
- `KnowledgeObject != Application`

No single container or alias may erase these distinctions.

## §4 Governing law

```text
No entity without identity and domain.
No capability without a bearer type.
No relation without typed operands and roles.
No transformation without capability, invariants, conditions, and a gate.
No evidence without a claim type, scope, and domain.
No judgment above its evidence and weakest required contract.
No knowledge object without provenance, rank, scope, residuals, and revision policy.
No application without case determination, membership test, blockers, and feedback.
No cross-science transfer without a licensed bridge.
No universal-coverage claim from three reference matrices.
```

## §5 Forbidden overclaim surface

The following inferences are constitutionally forbidden:

- `ThreeMatrices -> UniversalCompleteness`
- `StructuralValidation -> ScientificTruth`
- `GovernanceCertificate -> ExternalTruth`
- `SchemaFilled -> KnowledgeLearned`
- `EvidencePresent -> JudgmentCertified`
- `ApplicationExecuted -> CorrectApplication`

## §6 Relation to kernel

USM must consume, not replace, the existing constitutional kernel:

- `SlotGraph`
- `Gamma`
- `Rank`
- `Residuals`
- `EvidenceContract`
- `TransitionGate`
- `Trace`

USM is not licensed to introduce a parallel rank engine, a parallel gate,
or a direct evidence-to-certainty shortcut.

## §7 OCPC transition discipline

Every USM cross-slot transition remains bound to the existing transition posture:

- immediate predecessor
- declared addition
- gate
- identity preservation
- no-bypass proof
- rank ceiling
- visible residuals
- trace

No slot may self-license a transition outside this discipline.

## §8 Deferred surfaces in `USM-L0`

The following are explicitly deferred and remain non-executable under this law step:

- continuous learning engine
- repair/supersession/rollback cycle
- cycle/termination detection
- cross-science runtime bridges
- external truth correspondence engine
- universal coverage proof
- final universality certification

## §9 Next licensed step

`USM-L0` licenses only one immediate successor:

- `USM-C1 — schema/carrier-only surface`

No validator runtime (`USM-C2`) and no reference-matrix validation runtime may open
before `USM-C1` is landed and constitutionally tested.
