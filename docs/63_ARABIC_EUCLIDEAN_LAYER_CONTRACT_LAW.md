## §1 Governing principle

```text
Slot Geometry in Arabic is a layered contract system, not a table of cells.
No Arabic layer without the eight Euclidean questions.
No layer transition without licensed handoff.
No carrier becomes closure by title.
No closure hides residuals.
No linguistic candidate becomes meaning, ifādah, hukm, truth, or certainty by
shape, probability, weight, or need.
```

The governing layer equation is:

```text
Layer =
  ConditionOfPossibility
  + MinimumCompleteLimit
  + Opening
  + Demand
  + IdentityPreservation
  + Closure
  + Residual
  + LicensedTransition
```

```text

  شرط إمكان

  + فتح
  + طلب
  + حفظ هوية
  + إغلاق
```

law. They do not replace `SlotGraph`, `Gamma`, `RankLattice`,

## §3 The eight layer questions

Every future Arabic layer admitted under LAW-E0 must answer all eight questions:

| Question | Contract obligation | Refusal when missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Condition of possibility | What makes the layer possible at all? | raw material only |
| Minimum complete limit | What is the smallest complete surface? | incomplete candidate |
| Opening | What next surface does it open? | no licensed downstream |
| Demand | What does it require to close? | dangling request |
| Identity preservation | What identity is preserved across the layer? | broken identity |
| Closure | When is the layer minimally closed? | no closure claim |
| Residual | What remains visible? | hidden residual |
| Licensed transition | Which handoff permits the next layer? | forbidden straight line |

A branch that cannot answer these questions remains material, possibility, or
candidate only. It is not a constitutional layer.
